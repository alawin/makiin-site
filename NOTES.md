## Gotchas
- Never edit `id/`, `projects/` or `sitemap.xml` by hand. They are regenerated.
- Client copy is verbatim. Do not "fix" it.
- Logo and loader URLs carry `?v=2` to bust cached clipped copies. Bump the number if they change again.
- `wrangler deploy` is always followed by `git push`.
